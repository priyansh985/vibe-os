# ADR-NNN — Decision title

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded
- Context: What requirements and constraints force a choice?
- Options: At least two feasible alternatives, including a simpler one.
- Decision: Which option and why?
- Consequences: Trade-offs, risks, impact on interfaces and maintainability.
- Rollback: How to safely reverse or mitigate the change.
- Validation: How to prove the decision works.
